# Bug Descriptions

## bug1.py
- **Intended Behavior**: Remove duplicate elements from a list while preserving the original order.
- **Current Issue**: Removes previously added elements when duplicates are encountered, resulting in incorrect output.

## bug2.js
- **Intended Behavior**: Calculate the total shopping cart price based on each item's price and quantity.
- **Current Issue**: Adds the item price only once instead of multiplying it by the quantity.

## bug3.java
- **Intended Behavior**: Allow withdrawals only when the requested amount is positive and does not exceed the available balance.
- **Current Issue**: Accepts negative withdrawal amounts, incorrectly increasing the account balance.

## bug4.py
- **Intended Behavior**: Count all occurrences of a target word in a text file, regardless of capitalization or surrounding punctuation.
- **Current Issue**: Does not handle punctuation attached to words, causing some occurrences to be missed.
